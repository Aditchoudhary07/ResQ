About ResQ

ResQ — Right Hospital. Right Time.

This is a frontend demonstration of emergency-response coordination concepts. It is not connected to real emergency services, hospitals, or dispatch systems. All hospital data, recommendations, and acceptance responses are simulated.

For real emergencies: call 112 (India), 999 (UK), 911 (US), or your regional emergency number.
